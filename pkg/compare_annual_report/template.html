💡 Strategic Analysis & Key Findings
1. Massive Increase in Trade Velocity & Idle Capital Utilization

In standalone mode, VOO-TECL Combo spent an average of 78.2% of the time sitting entirely in cash waiting for 3 consecutive down-closes on VOO. In the 2-Strategy backtest, BB-Capitulation utilized that idle cash, expanding completed trades from 106 to 964 and lowering idle cash to 20.1%.

2. Priority Preemption Protected Primary Buy Execution

Across the 5-year backtest, 141 secondary positions were liquidated at market when VOO-TECL Combo fired buy signals. Rather than missing out on high-conviction TECL rebounds due to locked cash, secondary positions were promptly sold (frequently locking in profitable run-ups like GUSH +10.8% and U +16.9%) to fund TECL at scale.

3. Drawdown & Volatility Trade-Off

Because the 2-strategy account was actively invested in equities across 73 universe symbols rather than sitting in 78% T-bills, annual drawdown expanded during volatile equity pullbacks (e.g. 2025 max drawdown was 48.88% in shared vs. 38.64% standalone). However, in bull recovery years (like 2023), the shared account surged +70.76% vs. +23.12% in standalone!
